Workshop Options

Clarifying Expectations and Success

Transitions and Teamwork

A change in CEO or board membership creates an opportunity to reset as a governance team. Define what success looks like in the first year and beyond, clarify expectations, and agree on how the team works together.

Objective · Fair · Meaningful

CEO Evaluation

Develop clear criteria, select or customize an evaluation instrument, and agree on a process for meaningful, ongoing CEO evaluation. Focus on leadership impact, relevant feedback, and a shared understanding of ratings, with growth as the goal.

Learning · Growth · Improvement

Board Assessment and Planning

Organizational improvement begins with leaders who keep learning and growing. Assess your board's effectiveness, identify priorities, and create a plan for continued growth. Model the continuous improvement you want throughout your organization.
