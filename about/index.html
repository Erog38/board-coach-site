About Phil Gore, PhD

Experience at the table. Perspective beyond it.

Phil Gore is a researcher, author, speaker, and former local school board member. He holds a PhD in educational leadership and a bachelor’s degree in management.

His public service includes national, state, and local boards and committees. He co-edited Improving School Board Effectiveness and is the author of Confessions of a School Board Member: What Every Superintendent Needs to Know.

Through The Board Coach / Phil Gore Consulting, Phil helps leaders address the practical questions behind effective governance: Who decides? How do we communicate? What are we working toward? How will we know we are making progress?

Clear roles. Candid conversations. A common direction.

Your board’s history, responsibilities, and goals shape the work. Expectations and communication matter alongside policies and processes. The aim is useful insight you can bring into the next meeting, decision, or conversation.
