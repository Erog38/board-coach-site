Working together

Coaching grounded in your needs and goals.

01 / Explore and Understand

Through attentive listening and clarifying questions, Phil helps you explore your responsibilities, relationships, and aspirations. Together, develop a clear understanding of your situation and what you want to change.

02 / Clarify and Focus

Identify the decisions, relationships, and leadership skills that deserve attention. Together, establish meaningful goals and practical priorities connected to the outcomes you want for yourself, others, and your organization.

03 / Practice and Apply

Examine assumptions, consider different perspectives, and prepare for challenging situations together. Practice new approaches during coaching conversations, then apply them in your leadership between sessions.

04 / Reflect and Adjust

Review your experiences, recognize progress, and explore what you are learning. Together, adjust your approach and priorities as your understanding grows and new challenges emerge.
